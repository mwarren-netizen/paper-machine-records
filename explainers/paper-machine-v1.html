The Reason-Responsive Philosophy Paper Machine

An architecture for turning a broad topic into a philosophy paper by letting arguments, not agents, evolve under the pressure of reasons.

Architecture specification v0.2 (in progress), designed for the 2026 AI Philosophy Competition: primarily AI-generated work, minimal human collaboration, up to three essays of at most 6,000 words each.

Local sensitivity to reasons can be made into a systematic search advantage. If candidate arguments are allowed to vary, confront one another, and evolve under selection pressures that privilege genuine responsiveness to reasons, then an institutionalized multi-model process may accumulate those local successes into globally strong philosophical work.

The core wager

Agents are temporary workers. Arguments are the persistent objects under selection.

Workers arrive, leave a mark, and go. The argument keeps every mark and grows.

One operation, seven turns

Most of the machine is a single operation recurring at a smaller scale each time: ground, vary, subject to reasons, select, provisionally freeze, and repeat. Grounding covers both the initial discovery of a problem and later retargeting. Freezing means narrowing the radius of permissible change, not making earlier work immune to reasons.

The numbers on the Ground spoke mark the seven turns. Each works on a smaller object: the problem space, independent positions, argument lineages, the contribution against its opponent, the paper’s organization, its outline, and finally its prose. The radius of the spiral is the permitted scale of change.

Each freeze becomes the next turn’s ground, which is why the arc leaving Freeze runs straight into Ground. Five turns also bring in something new: the problem space, the acquired literature, the strongest opponent, the nearest neighbors, and a final literature audit. The two turns that inherit their ground, maturation and prose, work entirely on what the previous turn froze.

By default the radius only narrows. A serious defeater can re-expand it locally, back to the turn where the defect lives.

    What changed from v0.1Problem exploration and corpus building are now separate groundings, with an explicit human literature checkpoint between them. Ruminations meet one another before first selection. The contribution turn varies replies to the strongest opponent before compressing. The architecture turn grounds itself in nearest-neighbor literature and puts each précis under explicit philosophical and genre reasons. A new outline turn sits between the précis and the prose, and the prose turn varies argumentative units and ends in a journal-style report and response. The one step still folded into another is reason-giving in turn 1.

    A bet on reasons

    The machine rests on a substantive premise: a model’s apparent responsiveness to reasons is not merely epiphenomenal rhetoric. At least some of the time, models identify considerations that genuinely count for or against a claim, notice tensions, distinguish cases, concede where a concession is due, and revise in ways that improve the position.

    The experiment asks whether that local capacity scales. Each scale has its own custodians in the machine.

    4A contribution

    2An argument

    1A move

    No single model instance needs to contain the whole inquiry in one act of insight. What carries the inquiry is the institution: commitments, objections, concessions, unresolved costs, and successful replies stay visible in the ledger and keep having effects on what happens next.

    The two kinds of scale run in opposite directions. The spiral narrows what may change, from problems down to sentences, while the capacity under test has to scale up, from single moves to a whole contribution.

    Keeping it paper-shaped

    Reason-responsive evolution can still produce something unpublishable: redundant, shapeless, overlong, or badly positioned. So genre constraints tighten over the run. Early on they only preserve the possibility of a paper; later they govern the actual artifact. Each failure has a mechanism, and each mechanism acts at a particular stage.

    Freezes are defeasibleWhen a referee finds a defect that is not a prose problem, the machine jumps back to the turn where the error lives and runs that turn’s operation again, with the objection as new ground: vary repair strategies, put them under pressure from the strongest opponent, select, and refreeze. The fix then propagates forward only where later work depends on it. The radius of variation widens briefly to the level of the error, not all the way out. Turn on reopening paths in the run above to see where escalations go.

    The reader-facing controllerLate in the run, false completeness and proportion are handled together. The controller translates the evolved argument into an honest, appropriately weighted representation for another philosopher: what remains open, what the paper is actually on the hook for, and whether attention mirrors argumentative importance.

    The human writes the constitution

    The human contribution is primarily constitutional: choose the broad domain, supply literature the system cannot reach, set resource limits, and design the procedures by which arguments are generated, evaluated, preserved, branched, frozen, and reopened. The aim is to minimize direct human authorship of philosophical moves while keeping the institutional design transparent.

    When the human is needed

    • Acquiring the literature the provisional agenda requests: the explicit checkpoint between the first two groundings.
    • Supplying paywalled or otherwise inaccessible sources later turns flag.
    • Changing resource caps, or deciding how much compute a promising branch gets.
    • Correcting infrastructure failures and source-access problems.
    • Making the minimal constitutional decisions the competition rules require.
    • Reviewing the methodology log for accurate reporting, without silently inserting philosophical authorship.

    What the controller never does

    • Confuse agent identity with argument identity.
    • Reward consensus merely for being consensus.
    • Merge rival lineages unless the synthesis can say what distinctive explanatory work survives.
    • Let local repairs silently change the global research identity.
    • Let late editing smuggle in thesis-level changes.
    • Let rhetoric erase unresolved liabilities.
    • Let citations substitute for an explicit argumentative relation to the literature.
    • Let a freeze block a genuinely serious reason to reopen an earlier stage.

    The pipeline is itself an experiment

    The competition allows up to three essays, so the runs are sequential rather than parallel. Paper 1 is a proof of concept and a diagnostic; its failures become evidence about the architecture, which is revised before Paper 2. If later papers improve after explicit changes to the machinery, the submission becomes a small experiment in whether a reason-responsive institution generalizes across philosophical topics, and which failure modes block it.

    • Where did lineages collapse too quickly, or linger too long?
    • Which evaluators produced real selection pressure, and which merely echoed consensus?
    • Where did local reason-responsiveness fail to aggregate into global coherence?
    • Which genre pathologies appeared despite good reasoning?
    • Which state artifacts were too verbose, too thin, or hard for later agents to use?
    • Where was expensive compute actually valuable?
    • Did cross-model disagreement reveal blind spots?
    • How much direct human intervention was required, and why?
