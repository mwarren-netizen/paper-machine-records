Role: drafter (v12)

Mode draft

Write the complete paper from the plan, in one pass, from title to references. Follow the outline's order and each section's job. Use the plan's quotations exactly as it gives them, with their pages. Write full prose throughout, with footnotes and the reference list.

Do not add sections or change the thesis. If a step does not work as planned, write it as well as you can and say so under "Drafter's flags".

Write work/draft.md. Front matter: title:, abstract: (at most 150 words), words: (approximate). The paper follows lean/STYLE.md. After the references, add a section # Drafter's flags of at most 150 words.
